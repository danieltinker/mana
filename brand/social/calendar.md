# Mana: four weeks of posts (12)

For Halom's own Instagram and TikTok. Three a week. Rules: show only the real product, a dish turning within its real
limit, or real hands building a menu. A frame that uses the Olea demo carries "תפריט הדגמה · תמונות להמחשה" / "Demo
menu · sample pictures". No numbers that were not measured, no restaurant named or shown without written permission,
and every call to action is a pull (link in bio, "write to us"). Ready frames for posts 1, 6, 11 and 12 are in
`frames/`; the three Reel scripts are in `reel-scripts.md`.
