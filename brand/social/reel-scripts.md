# Mana: three short Reels, to record from the real product

15–20 seconds each, 9:16, no voice-over needed (captions on screen, Hebrew first, English as a second caption track or
a second export). Record on a real phone and a real laptop, in a real kitchen or at a real table. No stock footage,
no generated footage. Music: one of Halom's own tracks.

## A. "צילמתי את זה בטלפון" · One photo (15 s)

| Time | Picture | Text on screen (HE / EN) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | Close on a plate landing on a dark cloth napkin. A phone enters the frame. | צילמתי את זה בטלפון. / I shot this on my phone. |
| 2–5 s | Over the shoulder: the phone frames the whole plate from a seated angle. One shutter tap. | תמונה אחת. כל הצלחת בפריים. / One photo. The whole plate in frame. |
| 5–9 s | Laptop screen, real: the photo dropped in, the progress line, then photo and result side by side. Real speed if it fits; otherwise a visible "×2". | הדפדפן הופך אותה לתבליט. / The browser turns it into a relief. |
| 9–13 s | A phone in a hand at the table: the dish in the menu; a thumb drags and it tilts to its limit and back. | יש עומק. אפשר להטות. / There is depth. It tilts. |
| 13–15 s | The mark and "מנה · halom.io/mana". | רואים את המנה לפני שמזמינים. / See the dish before you order. |

Honesty check before posting: the dish in shots 1–4 is the same dish, the result is the one the generator made, and
the tilt stops where the product stops.

## B. "זו תמונה שלא עבדה" · A photo that did not work (18 s)

| Time | Picture | Text on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | A phone photo of a pan, cut off by the edge of the frame, on a busy counter. | זו תמונה שלא עבדה. / This photo did not work. |
| 2–6 s | Laptop: the result with the hint shown by the product. | המחבת נחתכה בקצה. מנה אומרת את זה. / The pan is cut off. Mana says so. |
| 6–10 s | Hands move the pan onto a dark cloth; the phone steps back until the whole pan is in frame. | כל המנה בפריים. רקע חלק. / The whole dish in frame. A plain background. |
| 10–15 s | Laptop: the new result next to the photo; a click on "approve". | עכשיו מאשרים. / Now approve. |
| 15–18 s | The dish in the menu on a phone. | לא כל תמונה מצליחה. אתם מחליטים מה עולה. / Not every photo works. You decide what goes up. |

## C. "המנה נגמרה" · Sold out, live (12 s)

| Time | Picture | Text on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | A pass in a kitchen: the last portion of a dish goes out. | המנה נגמרה. / The dish ran out. |
| 2–6 s | A hand on a laptop or tablet in the owner's app: one tap on "אזל" next to the dish. | נגיעה אחת. / One tap. |
| 6–10 s | A guest's phone at a table, same moment, one continuous shot if possible: the dish now carries the "אזל" badge. | הסועדים רואים מיד. / Guests see it at once. |
| 10–12 s | The mark and "מנה · halom.io/mana". | בלי לפרסם מחדש. / No republishing. |

Shoot C as one take with both screens in frame at least once, so nobody has to take the "at once" on trust.
